LLM Enrichment

Overview

This section documents how Large Language Models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) were used to extract, verify, and generate new knowledge about Palmyra's heritage. We employed three prompting techniques: zero-shot, few-shot, and chain-of-thought prompting.

Prompting Technique 1: Zero-Shot Prompting

Prompt

"What is the current restoration status of the Temple of Bel in Palmyra? 
Describe the restoration methods used and the authenticity challenges faced."

ChatGPT Response

The Temple of Bel, destroyed in August 2015 by ISIS, underwent significant restoration efforts from 2016-2019. The restoration employed Anastylosis techniques, reassembling original fallen fragments in their original positions. The primary authenticity challenge involves determining what constitutes "authentic" restoration when original materials have been damaged. The restoration team used 3D scanning technology to document the original state and guide reconstruction decisions.

Prompting Technique 2: Few-Shot Prompting

Prompt with Examples

Example 1: Lion of Al-Lat
Status: Successfully Restored
Method: Anastylosis with original fragments
Authenticity: Material authenticity preserved

Example 2: Arch of Triumph  
Status: Reconstructed
Method: Partial reconstruction with modern materials
Authenticity: Composite authenticity (original + modern)

Now describe the Temple of Baalshamin following this format:
Status: ?
Method: ?
Authenticity: ?

Claude Response

Status: Partially Restored
Method: Stabilization with selective Anastylosis of original fragments
Authenticity: Composite authenticity — original materials preserved where possible, stabilization materials used for structural integrity

Prompting Technique 3: Chain-of-Thought Prompting

Prompt

Let's think step by step about Palmyra's authenticity:

1. What are the dimensions of authenticity (material, experiential, interpretive)?
2. How was each dimension affected by the 2015-2017 conflict?
3. What restoration methods address each dimension?
4. How can we define "Composite Authenticity"?

Gemini Response (Structured)

Step 1 — Dimensions: Material (physical integrity), Experiential (visitor connection), Interpretive (narrative transparency)

Step 2 — Conflict Impact: Material destroyed, Experiential lost, Interpretive challenged

Step 3 — Restoration: Anastylosis for material, community involvement for experiential, transparent documentation for interpretive

Key Findings

Restoration Methods

Anastylosis, Stabilization, Reconstruction, 3D Documentation

Key Actors

DGAM, UNESCO, State Hermitage, ALIPH Foundation, Local Communities

Authenticity Dimensions

Material, Experiential, Interpretive
