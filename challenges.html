Challenges & Solutions

Challenge 1: Data Inconsistency

Problem: Different sources provided conflicting dates and descriptions for restoration events.

Solution: Implemented cross-referencing with academic publications and UNESCO reports to establish authoritative timelines.

Challenge 2: Vocabulary Gaps

Problem: Existing vocabularies lacked terms for "Composite Authenticity" and specific restoration methods.

Solution: Created custom vocabulary extensions aligned with heritage preservation standards (ICOMOS guidelines).

Challenge 3: LLM Hallucinations

Problem: LLMs sometimes generated plausible but inaccurate information about restoration details.

Solution: Implemented verification against Wikidata, academic sources, and UNESCO documentation before incorporating into RDF.
